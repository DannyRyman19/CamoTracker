# MW4 Camo Tracker

The unofficial camo tracker for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 on iPhone and iPad.

Please Note: This app does not automatically fetch your camo progression as it is impossible. You will have to enter your progression manually!

Camo Tracker keeps every camo challenge in one place so you always know what to grind next. Track Multiplayer, Warzone and DMZ, each in its own tab, with the exact challenge and unlock level listed for every weapon.

## Every Modern Warfare 4 mastery camo

Track these mastery camos:

- **Multiplayer:** Mercurial Drift, Polyatomic Reforged, Orion Reforged
- **Warzone:** Parallax, Damascus Reforged, Empyros
- **DMZ:** Chiral, Ripple Tide, Helio

Plus the base camo tiers on every weapon, and the 100%+ tier once the capstone is earned. Weapon prestige levels are tracked too.

## What to grind next

Every tab has a Suggested section that points at the weapon closest to Gold. Sometimes that is the last weapon holding up a whole category, and finishing it unlocks the Mastery camo for the set.

Tap the circle to mark a camo done, or tap the number to type an exact amount, which saves time on the long ones like 200 kills. Weapon levels are shared across modes, so you set a level once and every tab stays in step.

## Stats and progress

The Stats tab gives you completion per mode, the Mastery track for all three, and a category by category breakdown so you can see which weapon class is holding you back.

DMZ objectives are covered too, including Faction Missions, Exfil Contracts and Stronghold Assaults.

## No adverts, no updates needed

No adverts! New weapons and camos are added with no need for an app update!
