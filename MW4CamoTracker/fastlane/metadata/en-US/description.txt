Please note: this app does not fetch your camo progress automatically, because that is not possible. You enter your progress yourself.

Camo Track keeps every camo challenge in one place so you always know what to grind next. Every weapon shows the exact challenge and the level it unlocks at, and each game keeps its own progress.

FIVE GAMES, ONE TRACKER

Modern Warfare 4: Multiplayer, Warzone and DMZ. Gold, Platinum, Harmonic Break and Divergence, every mode's Mastery camos and the Pyroclast prestige camos
Black Ops 7: Multiplayer, Zombies, Warzone and Endgame, from Shattered Gold to Singularity, plus Cold Snap and Nexus Horizon
Black Ops 6: Multiplayer, Zombies and Warzone, from Gold to Dark Matter
Modern Warfare III: Multiplayer and Zombies, from Gilded to Interstellar and Golden Enigma to Borealis, with mastery badges and prestige camos
Modern Warfare II weapons: Gold to Orion and Golden Ivory to Bioluminescent

Pick a game when you start, and switch any time from the title on the Weapons tab. Coming from one of our older camo trackers? Import its export file and carry on where you left off.

KNOW WHAT IS NEXT

Every mode has a Suggested section that points at the weapon closest to its next camo. Each mastery camo shows exactly what still holds it shut, class by class.

LOG PROGRESS YOUR WAY

Full tracking: keep weapon levels and count every kill. Camos that open at a level unlock as you level up.
Quick tick: tap the highest camo you have and everything before it ticks too. No levels, no counting.

SEE THE WHOLE PICTURE

Stats shows each mode's completion, its mastery ladder up to 100%+, badges and prestige camos, and a class breakdown. Share a progress card when you hit a milestone.

THE MW4 DMZ, MAPPED

The full Hajin Tac Map: fortresses, compounds, UAV towers, key rooms, exfils, caches and more, plus your own pins
Guide tables for level unlocks, crafting, keys, FOB stations and missions
Read your stash from a photo or a screenshot: the app counts every stack, all on your phone

ALSO IN THE APP

Home Screen widgets for the game and weapon you are working on
Camo app icons that unlock as you earn them
Pin the weapon you are grinding to the top of every tab
Filter, sort and search every weapon
Report wrong data and the fix reaches everyone without an app update
In English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese

FREE AND PREMIUM

Camo Track is free, with ads. Everything you track is free in every game. Reading your stash from a photo plays a short ad first.

Premium removes the ads and adds unlimited stash scans, camo app icons, your own layouts and card outlines, up to five pinned guns, every suggestion, and iCloud sync across your iPhone and iPad. Choose a yearly subscription or a one-off lifetime purchase.

The yearly subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
