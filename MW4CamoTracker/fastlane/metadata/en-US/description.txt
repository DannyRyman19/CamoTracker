Please note: this app does not fetch your camo progress automatically, because that is not possible. You enter your progress yourself.

Camo Track keeps every camo challenge in one place so you always know what to grind next. Multiplayer, Warzone and DMZ each get their own tab, and every weapon shows the exact challenge and the level it unlocks at.

EVERY CAMO, FROM MILITARY TO MASTERY

Military and Special camos on every weapon
Completionist: Gold, Platinum, Harmonic Break and Divergence
Multiplayer Mastery: Mercurial Drift, Polyatomic Reforged, Orion Reforged
Warzone Mastery: Parallax, Damascus Reforged, Empyros
DMZ Mastery: Chiral, Ripple Tide, Helio
Prestige camos: Pyroclast and its variants, all the way to level 250

KNOW WHAT IS NEXT

Every tab has a Suggested section that points at the weapon closest to Gold. Sometimes that is the last one holding up a whole category.

LOG PROGRESS YOUR WAY

Full tracking: keep weapon levels and count every kill. Military camos tick themselves as you level up, and one level counts in every mode.
Quick tick: tap the highest camo you have and everything before it ticks too. No levels, no counting.

SEE THE WHOLE PICTURE

The Stats tab shows completion for each mode, the Completionist and Mastery tracks, and a category breakdown so you can see which weapon class is holding you back.

THE DMZ, MAPPED

The full Hajin Tac Map: fortresses, compounds, UAV towers, key rooms, exfils, caches, chemical storage and more, plus your own pins
Guide tables for level unlocks, crafting recipes, ingredients, FOB stations, keys and missions
My DMZ: your level, your cash, what to build next and a shopping list for everything you track

READ YOUR STASH FROM A PHOTO

Take a photo or a screenshot of your stash and the app counts every stack for you. It all runs on your phone: nothing is uploaded.

UNLOCK CAMO APP ICONS

Earn a camo and its app icon unlocks: Gold, Platinum, Harmonic Break, Divergence and every mode's Mastery camos.

ALSO IN THE APP

Pick the modes you play and the tab the app opens on
Pin the weapon you are working on to the top of every tab
Filter and sort any category by progress
Search across every weapon at once
Report wrong data and the fix reaches everyone without an app update
In English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese

FREE AND PREMIUM

Camo Track is free, with ads. Everything you track is free: every camo, challenge and Mastery, and the DMZ map and guide. Reading your stash from a photo plays a short ad first.

Premium removes the ads and adds unlimited stash scans, camo app icons, your own layouts and card outlines, up to five pinned guns, every suggestion, and iCloud sync across your iPhone and iPad. Choose a yearly subscription or a one-off lifetime purchase.

The yearly subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
