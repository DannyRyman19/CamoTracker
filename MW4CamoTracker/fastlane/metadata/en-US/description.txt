Please note: this app does not fetch your camo progress automatically, because that is not possible. You enter your progress yourself.

MW4 Camo Tracker keeps every Modern Warfare 4 camo challenge in one place so you always know what to grind next. Multiplayer, Warzone and DMZ each get their own tab, and every weapon lists the exact challenge and unlock level it needs.

TRACK EVERY MASTERY CAMO

Multiplayer: Mercurial Drift, Polyatomic Reforged, Orion Reforged
Warzone: Parallax, Damascus Reforged, Empyros
DMZ: Chiral, Ripple Tide, Helio

Plus the base camo tiers on every weapon, and the 100%+ tier once you have earned the capstone.

KNOW WHAT IS NEXT

Every tab has a Suggested section that points at the weapon closest to Gold. Sometimes that is the last weapon holding up a whole category, and finishing it unlocks the Mastery camo for the set.

LOG PROGRESS FAST

Tap the circle to mark a camo done. Tap the number to type an exact amount, which saves a lot of time on challenges like 200 kills. Weapon levels are shared across modes, so you set a level once and every tab stays in step.

SEE THE WHOLE PICTURE

The Stats tab gives you completion for each mode, the Mastery track for all three, and a category by category breakdown so you can see which weapon class is dragging you back.

ALSO IN THE APP

Pin the weapon you are working on so it sits at the top
Filter and sort any category by progress
Search across every weapon at once
DMZ objectives, including Faction Missions, Exfil Contracts and Stronghold Assaults
Full support for English, French, Spanish, German and Dutch

No adverts. New weapons and camos arrive without an app update.
