No account or login is required. All progress is entered by hand and stored on the device.

Ads and Premium: the app is free with ads (a banner on the Weapons and DMZ lists). Free users watch a short rewarded ad before each stash scan (DMZ tab > My DMZ > Read from a photo); if no ad can be shown, one scan a day still goes ahead. Premium (com.DannyRyman.MW4CamoTracker.premium.yearly, an auto-renewing yearly subscription, or com.DannyRyman.MW4CamoTracker.premium.lifetime, a one-off purchase) removes the ads and adds unlimited scans, camo app icons, layout customisation and iCloud sync. Premium is reached from Customise, the "Remove ads" link above a banner, or the scan sheet; Restore purchases is on the Premium sheet.

The tracking prompt appears after onboarding and is only used for ads (Unity LevelPlay).
